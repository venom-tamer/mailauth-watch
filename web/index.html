Questions

Why does the SPF 10-lookup limit matter?

RFC 7208 caps the DNS lookups an SPF check may make at 10. Each include, a, mx, ptr, exists and redirect costs one, and includes inside includes count too. Past 10, receivers return a permanent error and treat SPF as failed, which can push your mail to spam or get it rejected under DMARC.

Is it safe to check any domain?

Yes. The checker only reads public DNS records, the same ones every mail server reads when it receives your email. It does not connect to your mail servers or send anything.

Why can't you find my DKIM key?

DKIM keys live under a "selector" that only you and your provider know. We probe the selectors common providers use. If yours is custom, the key may still exist; check your provider's DKIM settings.

What does monitoring check, and how do I cancel?

Once a day we run the same checks as this page on your domain. If a record changes or a new problem appears, you get an email listing what changed and how to fix it. Billing runs through our payment provider, Polar, which emails you a link to its customer portal where you can cancel at any time.

Do you support my provider?

The checker works with any provider because it reads standard DNS records. We recognise these services automatically and show their live SPF costs.
