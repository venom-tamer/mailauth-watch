MailAuth Watch

Terms

Last updated: 2026-10-11

MailAuth Watch reads publicly available DNS records and reports on email authentication settings. Results are informational guidance. We do not guarantee that following them will get your mail delivered to the inbox, and we are not responsible for changes you make to your DNS.

Monitoring subscription

Monitoring is billed monthly per domain through Polar, our merchant of record, whose terms also apply to the purchase. You can cancel at any time from the customer portal link in your receipt; monitoring continues until the end of the paid period. We check each watched domain about once a day; checks can be delayed or skipped when DNS or our infrastructure is unavailable.

Acceptable use

Use monitoring only for domains you own or manage.
