Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

The free checker

When you check a domain, your browser looks up its public DNS records directly over encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS from Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), with Google Public DNS as a fallback. Those providers see the lookups under their own privacy policies. The domain you check is not sent to us or stored by us.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, we use a cookie-free analytics tool that records page views and anonymous events (for example, that a check was run and its grade). It does not record the domain you checked or your IP address in identifiable form.

Paid monitoring

Payments are handled by Polar (polar.sh), which acts as merchant of record and processes your payment details; we never see your card. To provide monitoring we receive your email address and the domain you asked us to watch. We use the email address only to send monitoring emails about that domain, sent through our email provider, Resend. When your subscription ends, we delete the monitoring state for it.

Contact

Questions about your data: use the contact address in your receipt email.
